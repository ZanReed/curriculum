# What NZ schools expect for junior maths assessment and reporting (research, 7 Oct 2026)

Three research passes, 7 Oct 2026, for D51 (printed exams and quizzes). Public sources only. Most
schools' course pages sit behind SchoolBridge logins, and the Ministry's Y9–10 info sheet and maths
rubric PDFs returned 403 from this environment. **Zan should download those two himself** from the
NZC reporting page.

## The national rules

- **Who and what:** Years 0–10 English and maths report against the revised curriculum from 2026.
  Years 11–13 and other learning areas stay on NZC 2007.
  [NZC reporting page](https://newzealandcurriculum.tahurangi.education.govt.nz/reporting-to-parents-and-wh-nau/5637158852.p)
- **How often:** written reports at least twice a year (Regulation 21).
- **Scale:** five descriptors (Emerging, Developing, Consolidating, Proficient, Exceeding), judged
  against year-level Progress Markers in six maths strands (number, algebra, measurement, geometry,
  statistics, probability). The end-of-year goal is Proficient or above. Below Proficient mid-year
  can still be on track.
- **From 1 Jan 2027 (amended regulations):** reports must include:
  - twice-yearly assessment results;
  - progress by strand;
  - the descriptors;
  - support for students below expectations;
  - attendance;
  - next steps.

  In 2026 these are encouraged.
- **Tools:** SMART, PAT or e-asTTle are required for Years 3–8 only. The Ministry says they are
  "not required for Years 9–10, however schools and kura may choose to use these tools."
  [Tāhūrangi](https://tahurangi.education.govt.nz/reporting-to-parents-and-whanau-guidance-released)
  SMART covers Years 3–10, with windows of 20 Apr–22 May and 12 Oct–13 Nov. Rosehill and Green Bay
  run it at Y9.
- **Mapping tables:** NZCER maps PAT Maths scores to the descriptors for Years 3/4–10. The
  Proficient threshold is 65.3 at Y9 and 70.8 at Y10, and NZCER says these are "not automatic cut
  scores". The e-asTTle table was extended to Y10, and e-asTTle is phased out at the end of 2026.
  No SMART mapping table has been published.
  [NZCER PDF](https://www.nzcer.org.nz/sites/default/files/downloads/PATs%E2%80%94Mapping%20results%20to%20the%20Progress%20Descriptors.pdf)
- **Judgement:** the descriptor is a teacher judgement drawing on several sources: rubrics,
  assessment results, observation and learning evidence. Nothing found limits the evidence to
  standardised tools, so a teacher's own tests count.
- **Senior qualifications** ([Tāhūrangi](https://tahurangi.education.govt.nz/senior-secondary-quals-finalised)):
  - timeline: 2027 is preparation; the Foundational Award replaces NCEA L1 in 2028; NZCE in 2029;
    NZACE in 2030;
  - grades run A+ to E, with C as a pass;
  - each subject combines coursework and at least one exam, three to four assessments a year;
  - maths is compulsory at Y11.

## Implications for D51

1. **The printed exam and topic quiz pattern is normal.** Botany Downs, Tauranga Boys', Selwyn and
   Auckland Grammar all run junior exams, and Botany Downs runs end-of-topic tests. D51 fits.
2. **Expect department-common assessment.** At several schools, tests appear to be shared across
   the department. A teacher's own quizzes would likely run alongside common tests, so D51's
   instruments should be usable as either the department's tests or a teacher's supplement.
3. **Report per strand on the five descriptors.** This becomes regulated from 2027. D51's per-item
   skill map already supports it.
   - The results sheet should give a descriptor guide per strand, not a percentage.
   - Mid-year and end-of-year results need different readings.
4. **Also map scores to N/A/M/E.** Some schools (Botany Downs) grade junior tests that way.
5. **Leave room for SMART/PAT windows** in Term 2 and Term 4 where schools run them. The PAT
   thresholds can sanity-check our descriptor guide.
6. **Y10 timed-exam practice matters more from 2027.** The Foundational Award arrives in 2028, and
   the new qualifications include exams.
